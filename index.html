IGL Spirits makes its own alcohol, bottles its own spirits, and supplies ethanol for India's fuel. Few companies in the industry do all three, from one harvest, in two plants, for two very different journeys.

Every drop, distilled in-house

Extra-neutral alcohol from Kashipur and Gorakhpur becomes whisky, rum, vodka and country liquor, bottled in our own plants.

One molecule

Spirits and fuel ethanol start as the same thing: ethyl alcohol, C₂H₅OH. Two carbon atoms, one oxygen and six hydrogen. What changes is where it goes next.

From field to fuel

Five steps, all inside one company.

01

Grown on the farm

The raw material for alcohol comes from crops grown across north India, close to the plants that process it.

2home states: Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand
02

Distilled in-house

The plants at Kashipur and Gorakhpur turn it into extra-neutral alcohol (ENA), the base for everything that follows.

379Mlitres a year of capacity
03

Stored, then split

Some ENA goes into the company's own spirits, some is sold to other buyers, and the rest becomes fuel ethanol.

3routes for every litre
04

Bottled for the shelf

Whisky, rum, vodka and country liquor, plus around 15 years of contract manufacturing for a global spirits company.

30Mcases a year
05

Shipped as fuel

Fuel ethanol goes to India's oil marketing companies, who blend it into petrol at the pump.

Top 10fuel ethanol supplier, about 220 million litres allocated for 2025-26

The plan the company has set out

  1. FY26: delivered

    Rs 2,801 crore revenue, Rs 492 crore EBITDA and Rs 244 crore profit after tax.

  2. FY27: grow the premium side

    EBITDA above Rs 550 crore, new IMFL launches, and IMFL volume doubled.

  3. FY28: debt-free

    Pay down debt completely, and expand into eight more states.

  4. Beyond: top three

    Become one of the top three Indian alcohol beverage companies across consumer segments.

Targets are the company's own, from its investor presentation. They are plans, not guarantees.
